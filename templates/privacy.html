Privacy

  1. Who made 2D Diaries?

    2D Diaries is fully developed by one person as a hobby project, the idea came in the middle of a business class, and ended up becoming a hyperfixation. Contact: support@2ddiaries.com

  2. What is collected:

    Email address, name and profile picture URL as Supabase stores these automatically, the only reason why this is collected is that each user gets their own random character each day, which is tied to their ID.

    What the app creates, the daily character draws and your gender preference.

    A session cookie to keep you logged in. In addition to this, Render (hosting) and Supabase (The database) log IP addresses and requests logs. This comes outside my control.

  3. What is NOT collected:

    No analytics, no ads, no tracking cookies, no selling of data. Just the bare minimum for your login and draws collection for the app to work.

  4. How your data is used:

    The moment you sign in, a random character gets picked for you on the daily, without repeats, and showing you a history of each character collected and when you collected them.

    None of the information is shared but the services below:

  5. Third Parties that process it:

    Supabase: Database and authentication.

    Render: Hosting.

    Cloudflare: Domain and DNS.

    Google: Sign in.

    AniList: The anime characters images come from their servers. Your browser contacts them, so AniList can see your IP address, as with any website.

  6. What's public:

    For now, nothing is public.

  7. Retention and deletion:

    Data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted.

    If you want to delete all your data, you can email me at support@2ddiaries.com and I will take it out. By deleting, all your information will be gone, counting previous pulls.
